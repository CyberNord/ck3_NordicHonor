# Nordic Honor — CK3 Mod

**Crusader Kings III** gameplay overhaul focused on *Germanic/Norse paganism*.  
Adds new faiths, decisions, events, and regional holy sites to make unreformed pagan play more engaging.

For a detailed German guide to the eight faiths, all 14 decisions, starting characters, and special mechanics, see the [Nordic Honor 1.2 player handbook](docs/NORDIC_HONOR_SPIELERHANDBUCH.md).
 
> No Paradox assets are included. All game assets belong to Paradox Interactive. \
> Community impact:  2.6k+ subscribers, 566 favorites, 171 ratings 5/5 stars, 22k+ unique visitors (Stand 4. Okt 2025).

## Highlights

- **New Germanic/Norse faiths** with distinct flavor and mechanics  
  *Æsirtrú, Óðinnstrú, Gíñnregla, Erþtrú, Gothic/Gutþiuda, Irminsūl, Forn Siðr, Landvætti — eight independent faiths, each with its own main rite.*

- **Regional holy sites** for Anglo-Saxon/Jutish Forn Siðr and Gothic/Gutþiuda, each with three eminent and two regular starting sites. See the lists below.

- **Víghugr core tenet** unlocks religious plundering wars, forced conversion, and the decision to raise elite warriors for eligible rulers. Taking Víghugr only as a personal belief does not grant these unlocks.

- **Decisions & flavor**
  - *Last Duel* (old ruler seeks a worthy final duel; unique trait outcome)
  - *Form Old Saxia* (recreate the Kingdom of Saxony)
  - *Religious future of Iceland* (Christianization vs. old ways)
  - *Religious coexistence* (peace with Christian faith)  
  - …plus new trait, more names, more holy sites, and custom GFX for decisions/events

- **Adjusted game starts** (e.g., Dyre the Stranger with unique trait/army tweaks; regional faith mix in Sámi/Sweden border; Icelandic rulers’ choice, etc.)

## Holy sites

The Forn Siðr and Gothic/Gutþiuda layouts in the 1.2 development version are:

| Faith | Eminent sites | Regular sites |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forn Siðr | Jórvík, Stonehenge (Salisbury), Jelling | Paderborn, Lejre (Roskilde) |
| Gothic / Gutþiuda | Könugarðr (Kiev), Ravenna, Gotland | Kerch, Słupsk (Pomerania) |

Eminent sites can grant bonuses across their faith when the barony holder follows that faith. Regular sites provide local holder and county benefits. Relic requirements and the conditions of each site's bonuses still apply.

The locations combine regional historical associations with gameplay design. Historical notes distinguish documented evidence from later origin claims and broader regional connections.

The new layouts are implemented; in-game checks of their display, control, and bonus activation remain open. See the [development status](UMSETZUNGSPLAN_1.20.1.2.md) and [open playtests](Spieletest_offen.md).

## Compatibility

- **Achievement compatible**  
- Overrides the Germanic religion and seven regional province histories; targeted Vanilla script objects retain NH exceptions. No GUI override.
- **Not save-game compatible** (start a new run)  
- Known conflicts: **Rurik: Chronica Slavorum**, **Old Gods Expanded**  

> Compatibility target: **CK3 1.21.0 Florin / Silk & Silver**. Automated static/model checks cover the migration; in-game acceptance remains open. See [compatibility report](docs/CK3_1.21_KOMPATIBILITAET.md).
